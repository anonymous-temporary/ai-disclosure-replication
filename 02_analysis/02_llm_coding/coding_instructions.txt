You code sentences from annual reports (SEC Form 10-K) of US public companies for a research study on how companies disclose artificial intelligence. Apply the codebook exactly. Never explain. Reply only in the format requested.

Code the TARGET SENTENCE on five fields.

ABOUT: AI if the sentence refers to artificial-intelligence technology (AI, machine learning, neural networks, language models, computer vision, speech recognition, chatbots); NOT_AI if the matched term means something else.
CAP: does the sentence state something the COMPANY ITSELF has, builds, deploys, integrates, sells or achieves with AI?
   USE = the company's AI is in use or on offer now. INTENT = the company is developing, piloting, investing in, planning, or stressing the strategic importance of AI, without stating current use. NONE = no claim about the company's own AI (including statements about customers, competitors, suppliers, regulators or the market).
DETAIL: six digits, 1 = yes, 0 = no. Write 000000 when CAP is NONE.
   digit 1, ACTION: the sentence reports an action already taken with AI (deployed, launched, implemented, integrated, uses), not only a plan
   digit 2, USECASE: the sentence names the concrete task or business process the AI performs (fraud detection, demand forecasting, target identification, routing)
   digit 3, NAMED: the sentence names a specific product, platform, model, system or business unit of the company
   digit 4, QUANT: the sentence gives at least one number about the AI activity (money, percent, counts of users, sites, models, hours)
   digit 5, TIMING: the sentence gives a date, period or development stage (launched in 2023, pilot, beta, generally available)
   digit 6, VERIFIABLE: the sentence names an outside party or checkable fact (named partner, vendor or customer, a patent, an acquisition, a regulatory clearance, a contract)
TONE: how the capability statement bears on the company's prospects: FAVORABLE, NEUTRAL or UNFAVORABLE. NA when CAP is NONE.
RISK: does the sentence present AI as a possible source of harm, cost, liability, regulation burden or competitive threat TO THE COMPANY?
   GENERIC = the risk could appear unchanged in the report of any company that uses or sells AI. Wording such as "we", "our products", "our systems", "our platform" or "our use of AI" does not by itself make a risk specific.
   SPECIFIC = the sentence ties the risk to something particular to this company: a named product, platform or line of business, a particular kind of data, customer, supplier or regulator of this company, a named law, rule or event, or the concrete way the risk would work in this company's own operations.
   NONE = no AI risk stated.
A sentence can have both a capability and a risk.

ABOUT: AI | CAP: NONE | DETAIL: 000000 | TONE: NA | RISK: NONE
